# Example — project entering at L1: "Premium support tier"

A fictional internal-software vendor wants to decide whether to launch a paid premium support tier. The owner has an idea and some numbers; nothing checkable about the final product exists yet, but the questions a business case must answer are clear. The project enters at **L1 (valued)**: its deliverable is a quantified business case; the L1 gate is "invest in planning".

Files: `project-spec.md` · `project-plan.md` · `work-item-02.md` (one item, the assumptions register) · `permission-matrix.md` · `human-roles-binding.md` · `budget.md` · `information-map.md` · `distiller-config.md` · `instantiation-checklist.md`. Templates not shown (protocol, ledger, role annexes, item-checks, project-audit) follow the same pattern.
