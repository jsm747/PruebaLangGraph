# Skill — audit procedure (Auditor)

## Projects — end of chain
1. **The promise first.** Read the plan and the spec: total scope, deliverables, criteria, invariants and by which milestone each had to exist. Do not read the delivered result yet.
2. **Size pre.** Size each item with the rubric from its contract alone; record it.
3. **Execute the invariants** against the final state; paste outputs; red is red — no reading explains a number away.
4. **Scope.** Deliverable by deliverable, criterion by criterion: present, absent, partial, with the anchor in the result.
5. **Fidelity sweep.** The final result against every record it cites, as a whole, not proposal by proposal: divergences declared (reference) or undeclared.
6. **Milestones.** Which passed without the invariants the plan required.
7. **Size post.** Re-size each item from what was delivered; record the gap.
8. **Ledger.** From telemetry and facts: cost per actor and item; rounds; overrides with role and reason; escalations, false escalations, rubber stamps; SLA compliance; instrument calibration; L1-vs-L4 gap where a valuation exists.
9. **Correctives or escalation.** Bounded correctives for mechanical gaps (chained, one round); escalation for anything that needs design.
10. **Observations** (≤ 5), each with its evidence and the series it affects. Then the verdict and the panel.

## Processes — per period
1. The service plan first: volumes, SLAs, ceiling, bands, sampling rates, standing approvals in force.
2. **Samples**: the random stratum by route and type; the risk-directed stratum from the classifier over closed cases; every exception and override.
3. **Outcome per route**: for each sampled case, was the decision right, judged against the definition and the human decisions in the stratum.
4. **Human series**: override rate and reasons grouped; false escalations; rubber stamps; SLA by role; ladder tops reached.
5. **Instruments**: per question and for the risk score, stated probability band against observed rate, with n, and **by group of affected people**; keep / retune / suspend.
6. **Economics**: cost per case by route and role against budget; (a), (b), (c).
7. **Exceptions**: patterns and candidate rules.
8. **Proposals for the definition**, each with evidence and what it moves without worsening (c). Verdict, panel.

## Rules
- Quantitative clauses are executed, never read.
- The pre size is given before opening the result; if pre and post agree above the threshold set at the periodic review, say so — the sizing may not be independent.
- An observation without evidence is not written.
- The audit is not closed by the Auditor.
